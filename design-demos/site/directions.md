# Direction cards: download page (brand-constrained: all six are readings of the approved notebook)

## 1 · Best designer: Irma Boom (bold) — "the thumb index"
- Borrowed: the book as an object read from its edge; colour on the fore-edge; content found by thumb.
- Hero: a notebook page edge with real index tabs (Windows / macOS / Linux / Engine / Limits) that ARE the navigation.
- Skeleton: one wide page; tabs stacked on the right edge; the active tab's page is the content.
- Boldness: the index tabs, big and coloured, doing the work a nav bar usually does.
- Form from content: a tab player's notebook is literally organised by tabs.

## 2 · Best designer: Oliver Jeffers (illustrative, hand) — "the storyboard"
- Borrowed: sparse ink drawings + handwriting carrying a small story; lots of paper; humour in captions.
- Hero: a four-panel ink strip: play the solo → drop it in → the tab writes itself → play it back.
- Skeleton: strip across the first screen, download note pinned under it.
- Boldness: no headline type at all: handwriting and drawings only above the fold.
- Form from content: the app's four real steps.

## 3 · Reference: Teenage Engineering, OP-1 field page (teenage.engineering/products/op-1, refs/board.jpg) — "the product, cropped"
- Borrowed (seen): product ~70% of the first screen, bleeding off the edge; one short lowercase sentence above;
  name + tiny boxed tag ("field"); centred short paragraph; light neutral ground; small technical labels.
- Hero: the real editor screenshot, huge, cropped off the right edge, with numbered ink callouts on real parts
  (metronome on the take's tempo, play-along lane, string/fret numbers, export).
- Skeleton: sentence + boxed tags left, screenshot bleeding right; below, a spec sheet of the three builds and three engines.
- Boldness: the crop: the app is bigger than the page.
- Form from content: callouts are the app's own features, numbers are the real sizes.

## 4 · Reference: iA Writer (ia.net/writer, refs/board.jpg) — "one button"
- Borrowed (seen): centred icon + name, two-line tagline, ONE pill button that names your OS, the app window huge and
  overlapping the fold; a full-bleed colour band further down.
- Hero: the app icon + the real home screenshot in a window frame.
- Skeleton: centred column; window crosses the fold; a cobalt band with the three engines.
- Boldness: restraint: one button above the fold.
- Form from content: the visitor's OS is the only choice that matters on arrival.

## 5 · Seconds wheel 19 → web #20 Kenya Hara White Gallery — "one object"
- Borrowed: white-box gallery: vast ground, one object lit, tiny captions.
- Hero: the blue pick, drawn large in ink on staff paper, one sentence, three quiet download lines.
- Skeleton: a single centred object; everything else below the fold.
- Boldness: emptiness.
- Form from content: a pick is the smallest thing a guitarist carries.

## 6 · Seconds wheel 19 → east #8 Sugiura Information Mandala — "the listening wheel"
- Borrowed: dense radial information diagram; many layers read from the centre outwards; small type in rings.
- Hero: the real pipeline as concentric rings: recording at the centre → separation → beats → pitch → notes →
  strings → assembly → exports on the rim; the three downloads at the cardinal points.
- Skeleton: the wheel fills the right two-thirds; headline and download stack left.
- Boldness: the information density of the wheel.
- Form from content: the order of the rings is the order the engine really listens in.
