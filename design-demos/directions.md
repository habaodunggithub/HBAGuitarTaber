# Direction cards

## 1 · Field sketchbook ("Wander the Horizon")
- Logic & source: reference 1 (user-supplied), read as a guitarist's field notebook.
- Borrowed: giant ink-textured condensed display set against the illustration's left edge; mono field notes with coordinates around the drawing; one accent (cobalt) used for the word that matters and for the notes; dashed path linking numbered waypoints.
- Hero asset: an ink line drawing of an electric guitar and a small amp, with a blue wash, SVG with a hand-wobble filter.
- Skeleton: huge headline left, drawing center-right, annotations on the edges, recent tabs as waypoints along a dashed path.
- Boldness: the headline at ~300px tall.
- Interactive: click the amp's lamp to switch the whole page to night (practice at night); hover a waypoint and its tab line draws itself in ink.
- Form from content: the dashed path is the real analysis sequence; the coordinates are string/fret positions.

## 2 · Watercolor plate ("Дом на Хлебном")
- Logic & source: reference 2 (user-supplied), a quiet architectural watercolor page.
- Borrowed: centered framed plate floating on a faded ghost of itself; small condensed serif title stacked with a tiny "into" between rules; ochre + indigo washes; pagination dots under the plate.
- Hero asset: a watercolor guitar headstock/body in indigo with an ochre bloom, ink over watercolor.
- Skeleton: one framed card centered, text left inside it, illustration right, steps as pagination dots.
- Boldness: restraint: one plate on a wash, almost nothing else.
- Interactive: the cursor wets the paper (pigment blooms where you move); dropping a file spreads a wash across the plate.
- Form from content: watercolor bleeding = audio turning into notes.

## 3 · Logbook collage ("Beyond the Tide" + "Nefela")
- Logic & source: references 3 and 4 (user-supplied), collage with HUD logbook.
- Borrowed: distressed condensed serif headline left, a tall teal band behind the hero, floating objects with cast shadows, HUD readouts (time, level, direction) in the corners, "find the X, click to Y" prompt.
- Hero asset: an engraved guitar standing in the teal band, vinyl records drifting like the whales, cloud/map texture.
- Skeleton: three columns: headline + logbook left, hero in the band, readouts + map right.
- Boldness: floating records that move with the cursor (parallax depth).
- Interactive: "Find the pick, click to strike": the strings ring (real synthesized pluck via Web Audio) and the VU readout jumps; records parallax with the pointer; a live clock.
- Form from content: readouts are the take's real numbers (tempo, key, tuning).
